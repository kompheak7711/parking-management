# V1-056 slice 11g-2 review record: reduce or waive a Late-payment Charge

Built 2026-10-25 in the cloud session on branch `wip/slice-11` after f4172df. Migration
`20270211_199_late_payment_charge_reduction` (head). Decision log DL-086.

## What was built

- `POST /api/v1/late-payment-charges/{charge_id}/reductions` under
  `late_payment.charge_correction` (the Owner and Manager default bundles only): a lower amount
  (0 waives the charge), one of three fixed reasons and a "What happened" text (DL-076 answer 4).
  The amount only goes down; a waived charge takes no more change.
- `app.reduce_late_payment_charge` (financial role only) writes a REDUCTION or CHARGE_REVERSAL
  correction and a row in the new append-only `app.late_payment_charge_reductions` (reason code,
  what happened, released value). A deferred check ties the row to its correction.
- Paid value above the new amount is released at once through the release records of migration
  `20261117_113`: sources reversed whole, newest first, the rest of the last one posted again;
  released Payment value becomes Credit of the charge's Customer (BR-CREDIT-014), released
  Credit value returns to its Credit (DL-076 reading (d)).
- The charge read names the reduction of each correction. The old corrections route stays until
  11g-3.

## Review round 1 (two read-only agents: security, money)

- Blocker (fixed, money): the command locked Payment rows without the Payment serializer and
  in two passes, so it could deadlock with a Payment writer. Now one id-ordered pass over the
  Payments of every source (a Credit application's through its Credit), each serializer then
  FOR UPDATE, then the Credits, then the charge and its Invoice, as
  `app.lock_settlement_correction_plan`. Test: a reduction waits for another holder of the
  Payment's serializer.
- Fixed: the old route could reverse a reduction made here and bring the charge back while the
  Customer kept the released Credit; it now refuses (P4603, 409). Test added, with cross-route
  key reuse (409).
- Fixed: a detected deadlock or serialization failure on this route answers 409, not 503.
- Fixed: `recorded_at` is never before the correction's time; "What happened" refuses white
  space at either end and control characters other than tab, line feed and carriage return in
  the API, the service and the database (`app.late_payment_reduction_text_valid`).
- Tests: the closed Final Settlement refusal is asserted as P5S01 through the financial role
  with nothing written; the deferred check asserts its own message; partial release of a Credit
  application; DELETE and TRUNCATE refused; a wrong Origin and an inactive actor refused.

Accepted and recorded:
- No test reaches the "paid again meanwhile" refusal (it needs a payment between two reads in
  one call) or the Deposit-paid refusal (only a Final Settlement applies Deposits, and closure
  refuses the command first). Both are plain checks after the locks.
- On a closed Final Settlement a Deposit-paid charge answers P4603 before P5S01; both are 409.
- Credit ids are visible to `late_payment.review` holders in the charge read; the audit action
  name is shared with the old route; the database does not check the capability (the service
  does, as on the old route).

## Review round 2 (two read-only agents: money and database, errors and tests)

No blocker. All round 1 fixes confirmed (the lock order is a subsequence of every other writer
of the same rows). Fixed:
- The lock-order test now fails with the old order: once the reduction waits on the
  serializer (seen through `pg_blocking_pids`), the holder takes the Payment row itself with a
  1 s lock timeout.
- A real-service test pins 409 for 40P01 and 40001 on this route and 503 for every other fault.
- "What happened" also refuses C1 control characters (U+0080 to U+009F) in all three layers;
  the command refuses lone surrogates (they would fail in the driver, not as a database error).
- The deferred check is tested with a released value no release row backs; the reduction
  detail no longer sets its own time zone (its time reads as the snapshot's other times).
- The route re-raises the same errors as its sibling routes; a comment no longer overstates
  where the replay check sits.
- docs/005 lists the route and every answer, including the corrections route's new refusal;
  traceability names the three new database tests and the new unit test.

Accepted and recorded:
- The reduction detail names only new Credit ids; value that went back to an existing Credit
  is counted in `released` and shown by the Credit application reversal.
- The charge read still marks a reduction made here as active, though the corrections route now
  refuses to reverse it; slice 11g-3 removes that route.
- The database judges white space by its locale, never wider than the API's Unicode rule, so
  the API is the strict side and the database refusal (P4602, 422) is a last check.

## Review round 3 (one read-only agent)

No blocker and no should-fix. Nits taken: a longer window to see the reduction wait, and the
first and last C1 control characters in the tests. Not changed: long lines in the migration

Also fixed after the full unit run: the request body model was renamed
`LatePaymentReductionInput`, because `ChargeReductionInput` already names the Final Settlement
charge reduction body and the clash changed that route's schema name in the generated forms.
